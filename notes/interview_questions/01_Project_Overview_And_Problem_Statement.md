# Question 01: Project Overview & Problem Statement

## ❓ Sawaal (Question):
**"Rooted project kya hai aur aapne yeh kyun banaya? Iska main maqsad (problem statement) kya hai?"**

---

## 💡 Aasan Jawaab (Answer):

**Rooted** ek all-in-one personal productivity aur life-tracking web application hai jo daily tasks, habits, calendar history, sleep aur long-term goals ko ek hi simple jagah par jorti hai.

### 1. Problem Kya Thi? (Why I built it)
Aam taur par humein alag alag apps use karni parti hain:
- To-do list ke liye alag app.
- Habit tracking ke liye alag app.
- Goals aur notes ke liye alag app.

Is wajah se log roz ke chotay chotay kaamon mein ulajh kar reh jatay hain aur unhein yeh pata nahi chalta ke unki daily mehnat unke baray life goals ke sath kaise jud rahi hai.

### 2. Rooted Ka Solution:
Rooted is gap ko khatam karta hai ek seedhi philosophy par:
> **Plan → Act → Track → Review → Improve**  
> *(Daily Activity → Habit / Task → Milestone → Long-Term Goal)*

### 3. Iske 5 Main Pillars:
1. **Daily Intentions (To-Do)**: Aaj ke zaroori kaam priorities ke sath.
2. **Habits & Streaks**: Rozana ki aadatain aur consistency track karna.
3. **Seasons (Calendar History)**: Puraane dinon, hafton aur mahinon ka review karna ke kya kiya aur kya miss hua.
4. **Milestones (Long-term Goals)**: Baray sapnon ko chotay chotay stages mein torna (Seed → Sprout → Milestone).
5. **Rest & Sleep Sanctuary**: Sirf kaam nahi, balki neend aur rest ko bhi track karna taake burnout na ho.

---

### 🎯 Interview One-Liner Summary:
*"Rooted sirf ek simple to-do list nahi hai, balki ek connected system hai jo insaan ke roz ke kaamon ko uske baray life goals aur sehat (rest) ke sath sync karta hai."*
