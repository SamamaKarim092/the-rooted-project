# 01. Project Overview & Philosophy

---

## 🌟 The Vision
**Rooted** is a personal productivity platform designed to bridge the gap between daily execution and long-term life vision. 

Most people use 3 or 4 disconnected apps:
- A to-do app (like Todoist) for daily errands.
- A habit tracker (like Habitica or Streaks) for routines.
- A calendar app (like Google Calendar) for schedules.
- A spreadsheet or notes app (like Notion) for long-term goals.

Because these systems are disconnected, people often finish their daily to-do lists while losing sight of their bigger dreams, or they set lofty goals without connecting them to daily habits.

**Rooted unites them into a single coherent system.**

---

## 🧭 Core Philosophy

```
Plan your day 
    ↳ Complete daily intentions (tasks & habits) 
        ↳ Review your consistency 
            ↳ Progress toward long-term milestones 
                ↳ Grow and improve over time
```

### Action-to-Goal Hierarchy
In Rooted, daily actions are never isolated:
$$\text{Daily Intentions} \longrightarrow \text{Habits / Tasks} \longrightarrow \text{Milestones} \longrightarrow \text{Long-Term Goals}$$

For example:
- **Long-Term Goal**: *Master Full-Stack Engineering*
- **Milestone**: *Build and Deploy a production Next.js application*
- **Daily Habit**: *Code for 45 minutes every morning* (Craft category)
- **Daily Task**: *Set up Prisma SQLite database schema*

---

## 🏛️ The Five Core Pillars

1. **Daily Intentions (Tasks)**
   - Prioritized tasks with due times and categories (`Mind`, `Body`, `Craft`, `Spirit`, etc.).
   - Clean separation between *Today*, *Upcoming*, *Completed*, and *Overdue*.

2. **Habit Growth & Streaks**
   - Daily and weekly habit definitions with target frequencies.
   - Dynamic streak engine calculating current streaks, all-time best streaks, and completion rates.

3. **Seasons (Calendar & History)**
   - An interactive visual calendar enabling retrospective inspection of any past date.
   - Shows precisely what tasks and habits were completed or missed on any given day.

4. **Growth (Consistency Analytics)**
   - Rolling consistency scores (7-day, 30-day, monthly).
   - Behavioral pattern detection (e.g., identifying habits missed on weekends).

5. **Milestones (Long-Term Vision)**
   - High-level roadmaps broken into tangible milestones with botanical stages (Seed $\rightarrow$ Sprout $\rightarrow$ Milestone).
